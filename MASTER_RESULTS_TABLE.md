## 4. CONCEPTUAL UNDERSTANDING ACROSS ALL ACTIVITIES

### Consistently Answered Correctly (90%+ of teams):
✅ Frequency doesn't depend on mass
✅ Period increases with length (T ∝ √L)
✅ Frequency angular ω = √(g/L)
✅ Magnetic damping inversely proportional to height

### Common Misconceptions:
❌ "g is directly proportional to L" (some confusion about inverse formula)
❌ "Calibration depth doesn't matter" (before learning lesson)
❌ "Angular velocity from finite differences only" (improved with polynomial method)

### Strong Analysis Quality:
⭐⭐ Outstanding: Bryan Hidalgo, Derik Herrera, Diego Atencio (understanding mechanisms, not just numbers)

## 8. Key Insights for Publication

### ✅ Pedagogical Success:
1. **Students understand physics concepts** — despite measurement errors, analysis shows solid grasp of ω=√(g/L), energy conservation, magnetic fields
2. **Self-debugging ability** — 2026 energy group identified calibration depth problem and tried fixes
3. **Multiple methods validate each other** — Energy (2026) and T²vsL (2025) give similar error ranges when setup is correct

### ⚠️ Improvement Opportunities:
1. **Calibration guidance** — Mesa 1&2 (2026) show need for better depth-of-reference explanation before starting
2. **Magnetic experiment** — Teams who reported "flat" β response may have had misaligned setup; could benefit from alignment checklist
3. **Form integration** — Students' written feedback (from forms) not yet analyzed; may reveal additional improvement points

### 📊 Data Quality:
