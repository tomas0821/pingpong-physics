## Student Feedback Categories (from analysis section responses)

### Key Sources of Error Students Identified:

**Experimental Setup:**
- Reference placement (depth error) — ⭐ Recognized by several teams
- Air resistance/friction at pivot
- Initial amplitude inconsistency
- String tension/looseness

**Computer Vision Program:**
- Shadow detection as false positives
- Camera angle/perspective errors
- Lighting conditions
- Frame rate / frequency synchronization with peak detection

### Student Conclusions:
- Strong understanding of inverse proportionality: T² ∝ L, ω² ∝ 1/L
- Good grasp of energy concepts (KE/PE conservation)
- Recognition that experimental errors compound
- Appreciation for computational tools vs. manual video analysis
